## Ask before paying

- What exact plan, account type, duration and region does the offer cover?
- Does it upgrade an existing account or deliver a separate login?
- Which features and allowances are included? Are API credits or other services separate?
- What renewal terms, account permissions and network fees apply?
- Where will payment and delivery status be shown?
